Capabilities

Engineered for Pure Performance

A modern native recreation with zero compromises: native Compose animations, infinite progression, and zero tracking.

Pure Jetpack Compose UI

Built with Material 3 dynamic theming and hardware-accelerated animations using direct lambda-offset rendering. Runs at silky smooth 60 FPS and 120 FPS high-refresh rates.

Infinite Number Progression

Powered by a 64-bit integer engine that never caps your game. Push past 2048 into astronomical frontiers: 4096, 8192, 16384, 32768, 65536, and beyond.

Single-Move Undo

Accidentally swiped the wrong way? Tap Undo to instantly roll back your last move, restoring previous tile layouts, scores, and turn states without penalties.

Jetpack DataStore Persistence

Never lose your run. Board layouts, current scores, all-time bests, and user settings persist seamlessly across app restarts and device reboots using modern DataStore Preferences.

Synthesized Audio & Haptics

Zero audio latency. Moves, merges, victories, and game-overs trigger synthesized platform tone frequencies paired with rich vibration waveforms from Android VibratorManager.

Keyboard & Controller Ready

Play your way. Full native support for touch swipes, physical USB/Bluetooth keyboards (Arrow Keys, WASD, and U for Undo), and Chromebook / tablet input devices.

Lifetime Player Statistics

Track games played, victory rate, all-time highest score, highest tile achieved, total moves executed, and lifetime tile merges directly from the built-in Stats dialog.

100% Offline & Privacy First

No advertisements, no analytics SDKs, no tracking, and zero internet permissions declared in AndroidManifest.xml. Your gaming data never leaves your device.

Native Android Sharesheet

Celebrate your milestones. Share your highest tile, final score, and victory achievements directly with friends via the system native share sheet with zero telemetry.
